3 Steps to Creating a High Selling Course for Beginners

Posted on May 21, 2018 | Written by Cheptiony Mutai | Category: CONTENT CREATION, VIDEO PRODUCTION

It’s no doubt that online courses are currently in high demand. However, the market is competitive: roughly **80% of the total income** generated from online courses is earned by the **top 20%** of course creators. This means the majority of courses are barely selling, often due to poor planning and mediocre presentation.

Does this mean top earners have better content, or do they simply understand their customers better? The latter is often true, but there's much more to it. When planning your first course, always have the end goal in mind and put yourself in your student's shoes. Here are the **three most important steps** to creating a course that sells.

1. Prioritize High-Quality Sound Production

Sound quality is arguably the most crucial element. Before recording, ask yourself honestly: **How much do I want to earn from this course?**

If you aim to earn $100,000 a year, you must be willing to invest in at least a **$100 microphone or lapel mic**. High-quality audio is a non-negotiable sign of professionalism. It tells your students that you not only **value them** but also deeply value the **content** you are sharing. Poor audio is the fastest way to signal low-effort and low-quality, regardless of how valuable the information is.

2. Build Personal Connection with Video Introductions (Talking Head)

For each module, include a **talking head** video of your face, or a spliced video of you speaking while writing on a whiteboard. This is vital for:

  • **Building Connection:** Seeing the instructor's face establishes trust and rapport with the student.
  • **Establishing Authority:** It positions you as the definitive expert in your field.
  • **Plagiarism Protection:** It makes your work significantly harder to plagiarize, as anyone sharing the video is essentially marketing *you*.

You can use this technique to introduce the topic, provide a welcome, or conclude a section, breaking the monotony of pure screen-recorded content.

3. Implement Professional and Consistent Branding

Never underestimate the power of branding your videos. This is how you convert students into followers and customers:

  • **Watermark/Link:** Brand your videos with a visible link to your website. If a student shares a video clip on social media, the link provides a direct path for new prospective students to find and review your work.
  • **Value Perception:** Consistent, professional branding instantly raises the perceived value and premium status of your course.

Bonus Step: Teach from Personal Experience and Offer Support

Always teach something you have personally tried and achieved **tangible results** from. In the initial chapters, share your personal story—the journey that led you to these great results. This authenticity resonates deeply with beginners.

Furthermore, own your course by showing commitment to your students. Highlight what they can expect and be available to respond to their questions. You could even offer a free weekly Q&A call to build community and ensure their success. This way, you not only earn more but also establish a fulfilling business doing what you love.

If you consider these steps, you will find it much easier to market your course with confidence, and your network will be proud to recommend your work.
