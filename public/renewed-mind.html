What is Reflection Friday?

Reflection Friday is a weekly YouTube series where I pause from the business content and go deeper — into the spiritual and mindset foundations that actually drive lasting success. After 15 years of building businesses, I've come to believe that external strategy only goes as far as your internal wiring allows. Reflection Friday exists to help rewire that internal landscape.
